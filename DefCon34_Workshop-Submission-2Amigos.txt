Topic(s): Workshop

Title: All About Stoopie InfoStealers: Malware Analysis for Understanding, Custom Coding for True Understanding!

Keywords: malware, malware analysis, reverse engineering, rem, custom code, infostealer, info stealer

Type: 4 Hour Workshop

Abstract: Learn to understand InfoStealers through hands-on malware analysis. Then REALLY learn about stealers through writing custom, evolved code. Current infostealers are largely rule-based and path-driven. They target known locations (browser stores, wallets, .env, configs) and perform broad collection followed by straightforward HTTPS or webhook-based exfiltration. This approach is effective but noisy, predictable, and increasingly well-detected due to its volume and recognizable patterns. The evolution is toward precision and behavioral stealth. On-host, this means replacing static file targeting with a lightweight, in-memory relevance model backed by a compact training corpus of high-value artifact patterns (credentials, tokens, configs, internal docs). Files are scored using metadata, tokenized content, and embeddings, enabling selective, high-confidence extraction. On the network side, exfiltration shifts from “what is sent” to “how it is sent”—embedding data into timing, flow behavior, and protocol-native patterns rather than obvious payloads.
